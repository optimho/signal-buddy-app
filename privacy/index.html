Signal Buddy privacy policy

Effective 29 October 2026

Signal Buddy is an Android app that controls and monitors a 4–20 mA injector over Bluetooth. This policy explains what the app does with information. In short: Signal Buddy doesn't collect, send or share any personal data.

What the app collects

Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else. The app has no account or sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no crash reporting. It doesn't even have permission to use the internet.

What the app stores on your phone

The app stores the following in its private storage on your phone:

  • The last injector you connected to: its Bluetooth address and name, so the app can reconnect to it. Tap Forget in the app to remove it.
  • Log files you record: current readings and events, saved as CSV files.
  • Valve test recordings: the measured valve position signal from each test, saved as CSV files.

This information stays on your phone. It isn't included in Android's cloud backup. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it. You can also delete individual log files in the app.

Sharing

Files leave your phone only when you choose Share or Save in the app. The file then goes to the app or place you pick, such as email or a cloud drive, and that service's own privacy policy applies.

Permissions

  • Nearby devices (Bluetooth scan and connect): to find your injector and talk to it. The app tells Android it doesn't use Bluetooth scanning to work out your location.
  • Location (Android 8 to 11 only): on these older Android versions, Android requires location permission before an app can scan for Bluetooth devices. Signal Buddy uses it only for that scan. It never reads, stores or sends your location. Android 12 and later don't ask for it.

Children

Signal Buddy is a tool for engineers and technicians. It isn't directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
