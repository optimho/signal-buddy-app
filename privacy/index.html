Signal Buddy privacy policy

Effective 30 October 2026

Signal Buddy is an Android app that controls and monitors a 4–20 mA injector over Bluetooth. This policy explains what the app does with information. In short: Signal Buddy doesn't collect, send or share any personal data. It also covers Signal Buddy Web, the version that runs in a computer's web browser.

What the app collects

Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else. The app has no account or sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no crash reporting. It doesn't even have permission to use the internet.

What the app stores on your phone

The app stores the following in its private storage on your phone:

  • The last injector you connected to: its Bluetooth address and name, so the app can reconnect to it. Tap Forget in the app to remove it.
  • Log files you record: current readings and events, saved as CSV files.
  • Valve test recordings: the measured valve position signal from each test, saved as CSV files.

This information stays on your phone. It isn't included in Android's cloud backup. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it. You can also delete individual log files in the app.

Sharing

Files leave your phone only when you choose Share or Save in the app. The file then goes to the app or place you pick, such as email or a cloud drive, and that service's own privacy policy applies.

Permissions

  • Nearby devices (Bluetooth scan and connect): to find your injector and talk to it. The app tells Android it doesn't use Bluetooth scanning to work out your location.
  • Location (Android 8 to 11 only): on these older Android versions, Android requires location permission before an app can scan for Bluetooth devices. Signal Buddy uses it only for that scan. It never reads, stores or sends your location. Android 12 and later don't ask for it.

Signal Buddy Web

Signal Buddy Web is the version for Google Chrome and Microsoft Edge on a computer. It is served by GitHub Pages at optimho.github.io/signal-buddy-app/web/.

  • No data collection: like the Android app, it has no account, advertising, analytics, cookies or crash reporting. It doesn't send your readings, logs or any other information to us or anyone else.
  • Hosting: when your browser loads or updates the web app, it downloads the app's files from GitHub. GitHub may record technical information such as your IP address, as for any website it hosts. GitHub's own privacy statement applies to that.
  • Bluetooth: your browser asks you to choose the injector each session. The web app can only talk to the device you choose, and it doesn't remember it afterwards.
  • What it stores on your computer: the log files and valve test recordings you make, in your browser's storage for this website, and a copy of the app's own files so it can open offline. Nothing is uploaded. Delete files in the app, or clear this site's data in your browser's settings to remove everything.
  • Sharing: files leave the browser only when you click Download. They're then saved where your browser saves downloads.

Children

Signal Buddy is a tool for engineers and technicians. It isn't directed at children.
